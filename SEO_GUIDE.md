### Step 2: Submit to Google Search Console

1. **Go to Google Search Console**
   - Visit: https://search.google.com/search-console
   - Sign in with your Google account

2. **Add Your Property**
   - Click "Add Property"
   - Enter: `https://sonaai.in`
   - Choose verification method (HTML tag or DNS)

3. **Verify Ownership**
   - **Option A - HTML Tag**: Add the meta tag to your index.html `<head>`
   - **Option B - DNS**: Add TXT record to your domain registrar

4. **Submit Your Sitemap**
   - Once verified, go to "Sitemaps" in the left menu
   - Enter: `https://sonaai.in/sitemap.xml`
   - Click "Submit"

### Step 3: Request Indexing

1. In Google Search Console, use the **URL Inspection Tool**
2. Enter: `https://sonaai.in`
3. Click "Request Indexing"
4. Repeat for:
   - `https://sonaai.in/about`
   - `https://sonaai.in/dashboard`

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

**If your site isn't indexed after 2 weeks:**

1. Check robots.txt: `https://sonaai.in/robots.txt`
2. Check sitemap: `https://sonaai.in/sitemap.xml`
3. Look for errors in Google Search Console
4. Make sure your site is accessible (not behind a login)
5. Check if your domain is penalized (rare for new sites)
